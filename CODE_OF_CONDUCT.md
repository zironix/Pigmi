# Code of conduct

Pigmi is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, never on people.
- Give credit and respect privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, spam, and
deliberate disruption are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `ziritix@gmail.com`. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers are expected to apply these rules fairly and explain moderation decisions when doing
so would not compromise privacy or safety.
